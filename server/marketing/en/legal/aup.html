Acceptable Use Policy

Because Zorven is a tunnel service, some uses are strictly prohibited to keep it safe for everyone.

Last updated: 09.09.2026

1. Prohibited content & activity

It is forbidden to host, distribute or facilitate:

  • Unlawful content; child sexual abuse material (CSAM); promotion of violence/terror.
  • Phishing, fraud or deceptive sites.
  • Malware, viruses, command-and-control (C2) infrastructure.
  • Copyright/IP infringement; unauthorized distribution.
  • Spam or abusive automation.
  • Unauthorized access, scanning or attacks (including DDoS).
  • Hate speech, harassment, or doxxing.

2. Resource & network abuse

  • Do not endanger the infrastructure with disproportionate load or abuse.
  • Crypto mining, proxy/anonymization abuse and similar exploits are prohibited.
  • Attempts to bypass rate limits are prohibited.

3. Responsibility

You are solely responsible for the content you tunnel and the services you publish.

4. Abuse reports

If you believe a Zorven subdomain or tunnel is being misused, report it with evidence to abuse@zorven.app.

5. Enforcement

On violation we may disable the tunnel/subdomain, block access, suspend or terminate the account, and cooperate with authorities where required.

6. Updates

We may update this policy; the current version is published here.
