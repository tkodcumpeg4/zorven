Cookie Policy

How we use cookies to run the site and improve your experience.

Last updated: 09.09.2026

1. What are cookies?

Cookies are small text files stored by the sites you visit, used to keep you signed in and remember preferences.

2. Types we use

  • Essential (session): required for login and security; cannot be disabled.
  • Preference: remember choices like theme (dark/light).
  • Analytics (optional): only with your explicit consent, privacy-friendly measurement.

We do not use third-party advertising/tracking cookies.

3. Managing cookies

You can accept or decline analytics via our cookie notice and manage cookies in your browser. Blocking essential cookies may break sign-in.

4. Third parties

Fonts may load via Google Fonts; the checkout uses Paddle's own cookies. Their policies apply.
