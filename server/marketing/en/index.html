Features

More than a tunnel.

From sharing your dev server to publishing a custom domain in production — one platform.

Instant secure tunnels

Expose any local port in one command with a valid TLS certificate. HTTP, WebSocket and long-lived connections pass through cleanly.

Your own custom domain

Bring your own domain like api.yourco.com; Zorven issues and renews the certificate automatically via Let's Encrypt.

Live logs

Watch every request in real time with advanced filters, status codes, latency and export.

2FA & security

Harden access with two-factor auth, IP allowlists and scoped API keys.

Team management

Manage with your team using organizations, roles and member tokens.

Remote terminal & screen

Secure remote terminal and screen access to connected devices from the panel — with live hardware telemetry.

Self-hosted, your data

Runs on your own server. Your traffic and logs never leave for a third party — fully under your control.

How it works

Live in three steps.

1

Install

Install the client in one command, or grab the desktop app.

curl -fsSL zorven.app/install.sh | sh
2

Connect

Sign in with a token from the panel; your device pairs instantly.

zorven auth <token>
3

Publish

Expose your local port; Zorven gives you a secure public URL.

zorven http 3000
