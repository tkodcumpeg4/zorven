About

Tunneling, on your terms.

Zorven lets you expose your local services to the internet securely — and it can run entirely on your own server.

Why Zorven?

Tools like ngrok and Cloudflare Tunnel are fast, but your traffic and data pass through a third party's infrastructure. Zorven offers the same convenience while keeping you in control: self-host it, use your own domain, keep your data.

Our principles

  • Data sovereignty: your traffic and logs stay under your control.
  • Simplicity: install in one command, publish in seconds.
  • Security: valid TLS, 2FA, IP filtering, rate limits.
  • Transparency: open source and predictable pricing.
